Privacy Policy

Thinkerbelle, operated by Arabelle (arabellescarf), Kudus, Central Java, Indonesia. Effective 9 November 2026.

Thinkerbelle is a private web tool that Arabelle’s content team uses to manage its own brand content calendar. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what information the tool handles when a team member connects the brand’s TikTok account.

Information we handle

  • TikTok connection data. When an authorized team member connects the brand’s TikTok account, TikTok gives the tool an access token and a refresh token, the account’s open ID and its display name (scope user.info.basic).
  • Video files and captions that Arabelle prepared itself, which the tool uploads to the connected creator’s TikTok inbox as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • Technical records of each upload: time, status and the publish identifier returned by TikTok.

We do not collect information about other TikTok users, including viewers, followers or people who comment.

How we use it

Only to connect the brand’s TikTok account, show which account is connected, upload Arabelle’s own videos to that account’s inbox as drafts, and show the result of each upload. The account holder opens TikTok, reviews each draft and decides whether to publish it. The tool does not publish anything on its own.

Storage and security

Tokens are kept in restricted storage inside Arabelle’s Google Apps Script project, are never shown in the tool’s screens, and are available only to the project owners. All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent information, and we do not use it for advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run our infrastructure (Google), or when the law requires it.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while the TikTok account stays connected, and remove them when the account is disconnected or access is revoked. Upload records are kept only as long as needed to run and review the content calendar.

Your choices

The TikTok account holder can revoke Thinkerbelle’s access at any time in the TikTok app’s settings, under the permissions granted to apps. You can also write to us to disconnect the account and delete the stored tokens.

Children

Thinkerbelle is used only by adult staff of Arabelle and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
